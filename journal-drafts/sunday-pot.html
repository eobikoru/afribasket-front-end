Start with the pot, then the shelf

A Sunday pot is rarely one ingredient. It is rice or a swallow, a pepper base, oil with some color in it, and something from the freezer you forgot to thaw. AfriBasket is built for that kind of cooking, packed in the United States and delivered to a US address.

The useful question is not “what is African food.” It is which things can live in a cupboard in Houston or Columbus, and which things have to stay cold on the way there.

What can sit on the shelf

Pantry goods are the ones that do not mind a regular parcel. Grains, flours, dried beans, garri, unopened oil, stockfish that the label keeps at room temperature, and the peppers you shake in by the spoon. Buy the size you will finish. A big bag is only a bargain if it stays good.

  • Keep oils capped and out of the sun. Once a bottle is open, the fridge is the safer shelf.
  • Move flours into a tin if the bag will sit for weeks. Pantries get warm above the stove.
  • Read the pack for allergens. Groundnut, sesame, fish, and wheat show up in places a US label might not lead you to expect.

What has to ride cold

Frozen meat, fish, and some doughs are not pantry items. Neither is anything marked refrigerated. If your cart has one of those, standard shipping is off the table. The order goes expedited or overnight, with a cold pack, so the carton is still cold when it reaches the door.

You can mix a bag of rice with a frozen pack in the same order. The cold line decides the trip. Pantry-only orders are the ones that can earn free shipping once they clear the threshold on the site.

A short list for one pot

If you are stocking from empty, start smaller than a feast. One grain or swallow flour. One oil. One pepper. An onion and a tomato from the store on your corner. A protein you already trust, fresh or frozen. Salt. That is enough for a pot you will want again next week. The second order is where the extra jars belong: egusi, crayfish, locust beans, bitter leaf, the particular cube your house uses.

When something is missing, say so at checkout. You can ask us to call, refund that line, or pick the closest substitute on the shelf. A stew survives a missing garnish. It does not survive a surprise protein.

When it arrives

Open the cold items first. Freezer food goes back to frozen. Refrigerated food stays refrigerated. If a pack is warm that should have been cold, write to the support address on the site with the order number before you cook it. The rest can be shelved.

Then cook the small pot. The pantry is not the point. Dinner is.
